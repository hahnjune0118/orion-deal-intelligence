# Synthetic Dataset Card

Version 3.0.0; deterministic seed label 20261103. Generation uses no random process and no external data, model calls, employer records, real counterparties or personal information. Intended use is academic preliminary-screening evaluation and software regression. It must not be used as actual company evidence or calibrated investment probabilities.

## Case universes

Northstar `nb` is an expanded independent research scenario, with FY2025 revenue USD 1,200,000, cost of revenue USD 360,000, OPEX USD 720,000 and EBITDA USD 120,000. Its balance sheet and cash flow reconcile; its reported gross margin classifies implementation payroll in OPEX. The later Aster contract concerns a new period, so future customer contribution does not silently restate FY2025 accounting.

Legacy `ns` remains immutable. Material scenario differences are deliberate and require separate namespaces: `ns` source financial truth has FY2025 cash USD 800,000 and debt USD 200,000; `nb` has cash USD 450,000 and debt USD 250,000. Legacy Aster contract is USD 120,000 annually; expanded Aster subscription is USD 240,000 plus USD 60,000 implementation. Do not join these cases by company display name.

Ridgeway Homeware `mf` has FY2025 revenue USD 10,000,000, reported EBITDA USD 1,300,000 and zero CFO. The USD 300,000 insurance recovery and USD 100,000 relocation expense lead to provisional normalized EBITDA USD 1,100,000; the truth of nonrecurrence remains an evidence request. Inventory grows 60% while revenue grows 25%. A physical count confirms book quantities but cannot confirm a USD 200,000 potential impairment or remove working-capital risk.

## Data layers

| Layer | Location | Meaning | Model visibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source bytes and parsed units | Case `documents` | What a fictional document reports at a particular date/version | Only stage-allowlisted documents |
| Structured state and events | Case `structured`, `updates` | Authored candidates and actual core engine contracts | Never generic model input |
| World assumptions | `gold/*_world_model.json` | Controlled financial observation facts and deliberately designed exceptions | Privileged, excluded |
| Evidence/claim/question gold | `gold/*_gold.json` | Self-authored labels and explicit acceptable request families | Privileged, excluded |
| Replay output | Evaluation reports | Measured deterministic execution of the approved event script | Evaluation only |

The source generator and world are separately represented; validation independently compares native table cells with financial-world values and uses integer arithmetic for totals, cash flow, PPE, equity, forecasts and contribution. Intended contradictions are individually named, dated and linked to sources. Exception metadata never makes an arbitrary arithmetic mismatch acceptable.

Every evidence atom retains document/locator/excerpt, provenance, reliability rationale, evidence independence group and available stage. All actual source evidence is `document_only`. Simulated counterparty wording is not actual external authentication. Repeated CIM/management statements share an origin group; repeated mentions are not independent Bayesian observations.

## Label provenance and review

Hypotheses are precise analyst-defined binary questions. They have no fictitious source quotation pretending a document itself states the analyst's threshold. Company-reported assertions have separate extraction gold with their actual source paragraph and exact excerpt. Core claim/evidence/question IDs remain stable and preserve the previous schema contract.

Top-3 and hypothesis statuses are authored from the scenario policy before replay, not generated by copying engine output. Status comparison, source integrity and finance checks are mechanical. Source relevance, alternative family matches, materiality and investment importance require human judgment. Gold remains `self-authored provisional`; no outside analyst review or inter-rater agreement is claimed.

Alternative question wording is accepted when target, period, requested artifact and verification criterion match a documented family. One predicted question can match at most one critical family. Unmatched or extra output is retained in denominators and cannot increase coverage indefinitely.

## Splits and contamination

Northstar is the public development example. Manufacturing is an independently authored industry regression example evaluated under the same ranking rule. It is published in the same repository and not a hidden statistical test set. Public gold can be memorized by a future model. A real generalization claim requires unseen companies/documents, preregistered prompts and blinded human labels.

Public Orion remains an empty verified-source manifest. No issuer facts were invented or substituted with synthetic facts. Actual issuer identity, disclosure period, primary documents and traceability must be verified before that case is evaluated.
