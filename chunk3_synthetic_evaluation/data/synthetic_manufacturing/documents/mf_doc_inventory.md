Recorded inventory is USD 2,400,000: raw materials USD 800,000, work in progress USD 400,000 and finished goods USD 1,200,000. Management says all stock is readily saleable and no write-down is required.

Finished goods older than 270 days have book cost USD 700,000. Management estimates their net realizable value at USD 500,000, implying a potential USD 200,000 write-down not booked in the historical statements. No signed clearance orders or independent valuation supports that estimate.

Revenue increased 25% from USD 8,000,000 to USD 10,000,000, whereas inventory increased 60% from USD 1,500,000 to USD 2,400,000. Closing inventory divided by annual cost of sales multiplied by 365 is 114.0625 days in FY2024 and 136.875 days in FY2025; this is a closing-balance diagnostic, not average inventory turnover.

Operating NWC is trade receivables plus inventory plus prepaids less trade payables and accrued operating costs. It increased from USD 1,600,000 in FY2024 to USD 2,600,000 in FY2025. Cash, debt and tax are excluded from this case definition.
