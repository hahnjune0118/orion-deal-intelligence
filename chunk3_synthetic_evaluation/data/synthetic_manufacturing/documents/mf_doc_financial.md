# Ridgeway Homeware Historical Financial Statements

SYNTHETIC DATA — mf_doc_financial — 2026-11-03 — version 1

Reporting period: FY2024 and FY2025

SYNTHETIC DATA. Fictional management accounts for a domestic producer of reusable kitchen storage and cookware. All amounts are whole USD. The statements are internally reconciled educational exhibits, not audited accounts.

FY2025 revenue USD 10,000,000 less cost of sales USD 6,400,000 and net operating expense excluding depreciation USD 2,300,000 gives reported EBITDA USD 1,300,000. Depreciation USD 400,000, interest USD 150,000 and tax USD 150,000 give net income USD 600,000.

Net operating expense of USD 2,300,000 comprises USD 2,600,000 gross expense less a USD 300,000 insurance recovery received in cash. Gross expense includes USD 100,000 relocation expense. Removing the insurance recovery and adding back relocation gives a provisional normalized EBITDA of USD 1,100,000. Insurance settlement and proof that relocation will not recur have not been supplied.

FY2025 cash from operations is USD 0: net income USD 600,000 plus depreciation USD 400,000 less USD 1,000,000 increase in operating NWC. CAPEX USD 1,000,000, debt raised USD 600,000, equity raised USD 200,000 and dividends USD 100,000 produce a USD 300,000 cash decrease from USD 600,000 to USD 300,000.

Management describes FY2025 reported EBITDA of USD 1,300,000 as USD 1,300,000 operating cash generated. The supplied cash-flow reconciliation instead reports zero cash from operations; EBITDA growth from USD 1,200,000 does not make EBITDA equivalent to cash flow.
