# Ridgeway Manufacturing Capacity and CAPEX Schedule

SYNTHETIC DATA — mf_doc_plant — 2026-11-03 — version 1

Reporting period: FY2025 actual and FY2027 capacity

SYNTHETIC DATA. Management plant schedule. Standardized production-slot equivalents are used for planning and are distinct from the product-unit quantities in the sales schedule.

Current practical capacity is 100,000 standardized production-slot equivalents per year. The FY2027 plan requires 120,000 equivalents. An additional 20,000-slot press has neither an executed purchase order nor committed financing in the supplied pack.

FY2025 CAPEX USD 1,000,000 consists of USD 600,000 replacement and safety work required to sustain existing operations and USD 400,000 expansion projects. Management initially describes the entire USD 1,000,000 as discretionary growth investment.

Management engineering logs classify the USD 600,000 replacement and safety amount as maintenance CAPEX. Supplier invoices, asset-condition reports and the recurring maintenance budget have not been independently checked.

Opening net PPE USD 2,000,000 plus FY2025 additions USD 1,000,000 less depreciation USD 400,000 equals closing net PPE USD 2,600,000. There are no disposals, impairments or revaluations in the supplied roll-forward.

Existing plant supports current sales volume without the proposed new press. The additional equipment is a growth dependency, not a condition for maintaining all current operations.
